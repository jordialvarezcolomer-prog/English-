> **[HOOK]** Did you know these things about GTA Six? Stay until the end… because the last one will blow your mind.
>
> **[1]** Number one. The very first trailer got ninety million views in just twenty-four hours. Guinness made it official: the most-viewed non-music video on YouTube in a single day.
>
> **[2]** Number two. Lucia is the first woman to lead a mainline GTA, and you can switch between her and Jason. But here's the twist: Rockstar actually considered *four* playable characters… before settling on two.
>
> **[3]** Number three. Leonida is reportedly twice the size of GTA Five's map. And Vice City alone? Said to be double Los Santos. Rockstar hasn't shared official numbers, but everything points to massive.
>
> **[4]** Number four. Crime only counts if someone sees it. In GTA Six, the cops need a witness… or an alarm… to even know it happened.
>
> **[5]** And number five. The police remember you. Your face. Your clothes. Your car. They're gone from the minimap, replaced by a search zone. Change your outfit, and the heat drops. And you can even watch your own escape through their cameras. In this game… the cops don't forget. And neither will you.
>
> **[CTA]** GTA Six launches November nineteenth. Follow for more.

Notes de direcció de veu: "ninety million" (impacte), "four" (pujada + micro-pausa), "twice" (èmfasi), "witness… or an alarm" (pauses curtes), bloc 5 en crescendo i baixada greu a "neither will you".
