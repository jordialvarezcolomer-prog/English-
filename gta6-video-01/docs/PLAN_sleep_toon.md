# Production plan — "5 h vs 8 h of sleep" (cartoon style, Alex & Daniel)

**A. Central idea.** Same training, same food, same protein — but sleeping 5 h instead of 8 h quietly drains testosterone, hijacks hunger, kills recovery and burns muscle instead of fat.

**B. Main curiosity.** Daniel looks wrecked from frame 1 (dark circles, yawning). *Why?* and *what is actually breaking inside him?* → The face reveal at 51 s closes the loop opened at 0 s.

**C. Opening-hook options**
1. **Split-face (chosen).** Line: "One of them sleeps five hours a night, and the other sleeps eight." Visual: two huge cartoon faces — Alex bright, Daniel with dark circles, heavy lids, mid-yawn; a red hand-drawn circle around Daniel's eyes. Text: "8H" / "5H" stamps. Sound: impact hit + yawn whoosh. *Why:* the owner-approved line + a visible open loop (the circles) paid off at 51 s; works on mute.
2. **Alarm cold-open.** "5:00 AM. Same workout…" Daniel's alarm blares in the dark while Alex sleeps. Text "5:00 AM". Sound: alarm. *Weaker:* no premise yet.
3. **Number shock.** "After two weeks of short sleep, people lost 55% less fat." Text "−55%". *Weaker:* gives away the biggest reveal too early.
